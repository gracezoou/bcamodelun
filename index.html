Scrimmages

Model UN Scrimmage

A scrimmage is a real committee in a low-stress room. You get a country and a topic, run actual parliamentary procedure, and chairs explain what's happening as it happens. It's the best way to get ready for your first conference.

**How a guided committee runs**
  1. Roll call"present" or "present and voting"
    5 min
  2. Speakers listeveryone gives an opening speech
    60 s each
  3. Mod caucusfocused debate on one sub-topic
    10 min
  4. Unmod caucusfind your bloc, start writing
    15 min
  5. Working papersblocs present their clauses
    20 min
  6. Vote + debriefchairs give feedback to every del
    15 min

Academy Model United Nations is BCA's two-day commuter conference, run entirely by our students since 1999. Last year AMUN XXVII opened with keynote remarks from U.S. Representative Josh Gottheimer and Fair Lawn Mayor Cristina Cutrone.

We also run JAMUN, a one-day conference that introduces middle schoolers to Model UN.

Register at academymodelun.org@academymodelun
